# Fluted panel TV attachment

Concept mockups for replacing the freestanding dark-wood TV pillar in the living room with a **vertical fluted wood panel** that the TV mounts onto.

## Design intent

- **Attachment:** TV mounts flush to the fluted panel (panel hides bracket + cables).
- **Profile:** Classic millwork fluting — vertical half-round reeds (not flat battens).
- **Height:** Floor to underside of stair soffit / landing.
- **Media niche:** Recessed shelf below the TV for router / STB (same gear as today).
- **Stair relationship:** Panel sits in the TV nook; railing can stay visible on the right.

## Rough build notes

- Panel width ≈ TV width + 150–250 mm each side.
- Flute pitch ≈ 20–30 mm (half-round reeds).
- Use a reinforced mounting zone behind the TV (ply / aluminum plate) so VESA hardware does not crush thin flutes.
- Route power + HDMI in a vertical chase behind the panel into the niche.
